How the numbers are worked out

  • A premium counts as paid once its due date is today or earlier.
  • Payments fall on the same day of the month as the start date, or the last day of a shorter month.
  • Yearly increases apply from each policy anniversary.
  • Policies with no maturity date, such as health cover, are projected to the year set in Settings.
  • Implied return compares all premiums with the maturity benefit you enter. It ignores the value of cover and tax benefits.
  • Your data stays in this browser only. Use Backup to keep a copy.
